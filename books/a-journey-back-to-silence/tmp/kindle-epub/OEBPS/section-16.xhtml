About the Author

Sabino Pereira is an independent creator building original worlds through books, music and meaningful experiences. Under SP, he writes reflective non-fiction and stories exploring identity, faith, healing, relationships and the quiet moments that shape us. Under RB — Reira Bin, he creates cinematic music designed to evoke emotion, memory and imagination.

A Journey Back to Silence brings those two practices together: words by Sabino Pereira and a companion instrumental album by Reira Bin.
