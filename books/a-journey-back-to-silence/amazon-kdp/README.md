# Amazon KDP Upload Package

## Paperback

Recommended KDP settings:

- Language: English
- Trim size: 6 × 9 in
- Interior: Black & white
- Paper: Cream
- Bleed: No bleed
- Cover finish: Matte
- Reading direction: Left to right

Upload:

- `paperback/interior/a-journey-back-to-silence-paperback-interior-kdp.pdf`
- `paperback/cover/a-journey-back-to-silence-paperback-cover-kdp.pdf`

Production measurements:

- Interior: 38 pages, 6 × 9 in
- Spine: 0.095 in (38 × 0.0025 in for cream paper)
- Full cover: 12.345 × 9.25 in
- Spine text: None; KDP does not allow spine text below 79 pages
- Barcode: clear reserved area on the lower-right portion of the back cover

The front-cover artwork contains a fine border close to the edge. This is intentionally retained from the approved final cover. Because KDP permits normal trimming variance, inspect that border carefully in Print Previewer and on the first physical proof.

## Kindle eBook

Upload:

- Manuscript: `ebook/a-journey-back-to-silence-kindle.epub`
- Cover: `ebook/a-journey-back-to-silence-kindle-cover.jpg`

The EPUB is reflowable and contains:

- EPUB 3 metadata;
- one recognised cover image;
- title and copyright pages;
- visible and logical clickable tables of contents;
- eight passages with navigable headings;
- no page numbers, headers or footers;
- small-caps opening words instead of fragile drop caps;
- reader-controlled body typography.

Always complete final device checks in Amazon's Kindle Previewer and final print checks in KDP Print Previewer before publishing.
