# Production Decisions Register

## Locked

- Music identity: RB / Reira Bin.
- Book identity: SP / Sabino Pereira.
- Language: English.
- Voice: intimate narrative essay; natural prose; emotionally restrained.
- Twelve musical sections: eleven chapters plus epilogue.
- Memory Fragments connect the book to the album.
- The supplied “A Memory Book” cover is the official book-cover direction.
- Final page: “One day, someone will remember today. Make it a beautiful memory.”

## Provisional

- 6 × 9 in portrait format.
- Hardcover matte collector edition.
- 48–64 page editorial target, subject to print-platform minimums.
- Warm photographic still-life illustrations with analogue texture.
- Cormorant Garamond or EB Garamond for display; EB Garamond, Source Serif 4, or Libre Baskerville for body.
- No QR codes or music links in the first premium eBook edition.
