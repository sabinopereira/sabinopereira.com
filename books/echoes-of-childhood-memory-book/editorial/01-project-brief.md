# Editorial Project Brief

## Identity

**Title:** *Echoes of Childhood*  
**Subtitle:** *A Memory Book*  
**Cover line:** *The days we didn't know we'd miss.*  
**Music:** RB / Reira Bin  
**Written by:** SP / Sabino Pereira

## Purpose

An intimate visual essay collection about universal childhood memories. The book accompanies the album without explaining it. Each chapter opens a space in which readers can recognise their own memories.

## Editorial promise

The prose must feel natural, continuous, mature, and emotionally precise. It must never become poetry disguised as prose, forced sentimentality, abstract philosophy, or a prescriptive account of what childhood should have been.

## Intended experience

- Read slowly, ideally with the corresponding music.
- Revisited non-linearly throughout the year.
- Suitable for display as a premium coffee-table object.
- Personal enough to feel intimate; universal enough to cross cultures and generations.
- Honest about joyful, difficult, incomplete, and non-traditional childhoods.

## Chapter architecture

1. Full-page opening image or found object.
2. Chapter number and title.
3. Short italic subtitle.
4. The exact Memory Fragment used in the album narration.
5. Two to four pages of prose.
6. Deliberate white space and an unhurried final line.
7. Discreet listening cue for the corresponding track.

## Book architecture

1. Cover
2. Half title
3. Title page
4. Copyright and credits
5. Introduction
6. Before You Begin
7. Contents
8. Chapters One to Eleven
9. Epilogue — Home Was Never a Place
10. Blank breathing page
11. Acknowledgements
12. Blank breathing page
13. Final page: “One day, someone will remember today. Make it a beautiful memory.”

>

This replaces “The End? Or just another memory waiting to happen...” as the final page.
