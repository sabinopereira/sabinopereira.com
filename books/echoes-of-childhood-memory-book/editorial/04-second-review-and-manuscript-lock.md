## Cooling-off rule

Do not alter working prose for several weeks merely to make it sound different. During that interval, record only:

- factual or continuity errors;
- wording that fails when read aloud with the music;
- passages that create an unintended emotional response;
- objective language inconsistencies;
- layout problems revealed by the illustration programme.

After the cooling-off period, perform one copy-edit and one proofread. Do not reopen structural revision unless new evidence justifies it.
