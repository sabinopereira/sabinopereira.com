Sabino Pereira

Writer of short books about human behaviour, modern pressure, and the small performances we keep repeating. Satire when it helps. Reflection when it doesn't.

Reira Bin is the music project of Sabino Pereira. He writes books and records the albums that go with them, moving between cinematic instrumentals, faith, pop and Spanish songwriting. One rule runs through all of it: every word, silence and sound has to serve the feeling.
