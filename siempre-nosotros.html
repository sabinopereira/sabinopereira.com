The story

Love, after the first intensity.

Siempre Nosotros is an EP about love lived inside a home.

It is not about perfect relationships, impossible promises, or grand cinematic gestures. It is about coffee forgotten on the table, the sound of keys at the door, an empty side of the bed, the silence after an argument, and the choice to move closer again.

Across six songs, the EP follows different moments in an adult relationship: discovering that someone has become home, sharing the calm of an ordinary morning, waiting for the person you love to return, feeling their absence for one night, and learning that being right is not always more important than caring for the relationship.

It is an intimate, warm, and human project—a celebration of the kind of love that lives not only in the big moments, but in habits, small gestures, and the daily decision to keep choosing “us.”

Why Spanish?

The language this story needed.

I chose Spanish because this story needed a language that felt close, warm, and naturally musical.

Spanish allowed me to write these songs with a different kind of delicacy. There is a particular intimacy in the way the language expresses longing, care, home, and closeness. The words sound gentle without losing their strength, and they sit naturally within the project’s acoustic, indie-folk, and alternative sound.

It was also a way to explore a new creative space as REIRA BIN. This is not about leaving my identity behind or trying to sound like someone else. It is about allowing each project to find the language that best serves its emotion.

Siempre Nosotros was born in Spanish because these songs did not ask for grand speeches. They asked for simple phrases, breath, silence, and truth.

“Real love was never one perfect moment. It was all the times we chose us again.”
