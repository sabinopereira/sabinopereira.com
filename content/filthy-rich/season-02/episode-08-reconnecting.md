FILTHY RICH

Season Two — The Relationship

Episode 08 — Reconnecting

Claire and Thomas had decided to reconnect.

The decision came after three months of speaking mostly about logistics.

Who was buying milk.

Who was collecting the dry cleaning.

Whether the electricity bill had been paid.

Love remained present.

It had simply moved into administration.

Claire found the retreat online.

A Weekend to Rediscover Each Other.

No phones.

No distractions.

Guided intimacy.

Organic meals.

Private cabins.

Thomas looked at the price.

“For two nights?”

“It’s an investment.”

“In what?”

“Us.”

Thomas looked at the total again.

Apparently, us required a deposit.

They arrived on Friday afternoon.

The property was surrounded by trees and silence.

The silence had rules.

At reception, a woman named Willow welcomed them.

She wore linen.

Everything at the retreat wore linen.

“Phones go in here,” she said.

She handed them a wooden box.

Claire placed hers inside.

Thomas hesitated.

“Completely off?”

“That’s the invitation.”

“What if there’s an emergency?”

Willow smiled.

“Most emergencies begin by believing everything is one.”

Thomas turned the phone off.

Claire watched.

It was the most commitment she had seen from him all month.

Their cabin had no television.

No Wi-Fi.

No clock.

A small card on the bed read:

There is nowhere else you need to be.

Thomas checked his wrist.

He had forgotten his watch.

“At least that’s accurate,” he said.

The first exercise began at five.

Six couples sat in a circle.

Willow placed a candle in the center.

“We’re going to begin with uninterrupted eye contact.”

“For how long?” Thomas asked.

“Until you stop counting.”

Claire looked at him.

He looked back.

For the first ten seconds, they smiled.

At twenty, Thomas blinked.

At thirty, Claire became aware of her face.

At forty, Thomas looked at the candle.

“Stay with your partner,” Willow said.

“I am.”

“With your eyes.”

Thomas returned.

At one minute, Claire laughed.

“Sorry.”

“What?”

“Nothing.”

“Why are you laughing?”

“You look uncomfortable.”

“I am uncomfortable.”

Willow leaned forward.

“Can discomfort be intimacy?”

Thomas looked at Claire.

“Apparently.”

That evening, they ate lentil stew at a shared table.

The food had no salt.

“Mindful eating,” Claire whispered.

Thomas chewed carefully.

“I’m mindfully noticing the absence of flavor.”

Across from them, another couple held hands between bites.

Claire noticed.

Thomas noticed Claire noticing.

He placed his hand over hers.

It looked correct.

She smiled.

He kept it there until the waiter brought dessert.

The next morning, they attended a workshop called Speaking From the Heart.

Each person had three minutes.

No interruption.

No defense.

No solutions.

Claire went first.

“I miss you,” she said.

Thomas looked at her.

“I’m here.”

Willow raised one finger.

No interruption.

Thomas nodded.

Claire continued.

“I know you’re physically here. I mean I miss feeling like we choose each other.”

Thomas looked down.

“I miss talking about things that aren’t tasks.”

The timer rang.

Thomas took a breath.

“My turn?”

Willow nodded.

“I feel like nothing I do is enough.”

Claire’s face changed.

Thomas continued quickly.

“When I come home, there’s always something I forgot. Something I didn’t notice. Something I should have understood without being told.”

Claire opened her mouth.

Willow raised the finger again.

Claire closed it.

“I know I’ve been distant,” Thomas said. “But sometimes distance feels safer than getting it wrong.”

The timer rang.

The room remained quiet.

Claire looked at him.

For once, there was no immediate answer available.

After lunch, they walked through the forest.

No guide.

No exercise.

No candle.

Just trees.

Thomas kicked a stone along the path.

Claire watched it roll.

“You really feel that way?”

“Yes.”

“Why didn’t you say it?”

“I did.”

“When?”

“Usually after you were already angry.”

Claire considered this.

“That may not have been the best timing.”

“No.”

They walked another few minutes.

“I do correct you a lot,” she said.

“Yes.”

“You could have softened that.”

“I thought we were reconnecting.”

Claire laughed.

Thomas did too.

It was the first moment of the weekend that had not been scheduled.

That evening, they skipped the workshop on conscious touch.

They stayed in the cabin.

Thomas found a deck of cards in a drawer.

They played badly.

Claire accused him of cheating.

Thomas denied it without emotional vocabulary.

They ordered pizza from a restaurant twelve miles away.

The retreat discouraged outside food.

Thomas met the delivery driver at the gate.

When he returned, Claire had arranged two plates.

“No photograph?” he asked.

“My phone is in a box.”

“Right.”

They ate on the floor.

The pizza had too much salt.

It was perfect.

On Sunday morning, Willow returned their phones.

Claire’s screen lit up with forty-three notifications.

Thomas had twenty-eight.

They both looked.

Then looked at each other.

“Breakfast first?” Claire asked.

Thomas turned his phone face down.

“Yes.”

At breakfast, another couple asked whether the retreat had helped.

Claire looked at Thomas.

Thomas looked at the organic oatmeal.

“I think so,” Claire said.

On the drive home, they stopped at a gas station.

Thomas bought coffee.

Claire checked Instagram.

The retreat had tagged them in a photograph from the eye-contact exercise.

They looked serious.

Connected.

Almost transformed.

The caption read:

Two people choosing presence.

Claire showed Thomas.

“Should I share it?”

Thomas looked at the road.

“Did we choose presence?”

“For parts of it.”

“Then it’s accurate.”

Claire reposted the photograph.

By the time they arrived home, the story had received dozens of replies.

Love this journey for you.

You two are goals.

Healthy love.

Claire placed the phones on the kitchen counter.

The laundry basket was full.

The electricity bill remained unpaid.

The milk had expired.

Thomas looked around.

“So.”

“So.”

They stood in the quiet apartment.

No trees.

No Willow.

No candles.

Nothing guiding the intimacy.

Thomas picked up the electricity bill.

Claire took out the milk.

They began again with the logistics.
