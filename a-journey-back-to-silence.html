Arriving September 5, 2026 View the tracklist

The album

Quiet is not emptiness.

A Journey Back to Silence is about the slow return to yourself after the world has become too loud. The ocean is its first language: restless on the surface, steady underneath.

Across eight instrumental chapters, the album moves from the returning tide into morning, rooms opened by light, echoes carried between waves, and the emotional weight we hold when words are no longer useful.

It ends where land gives way and rain has passed—not with disappearance, but with clarity. The silence here is not an escape. It is what remains when everything unnecessary has finally fallen away.
