## Caption

Most scams do not begin with technology.

They begin with pressure.

A message arrives. It sounds urgent. Something will be blocked, lost or cancelled if we do not act now.

That pressure is the mechanism. It gives fear no time to become a question.

One habit matters more than technical confidence: pause before you respond.

I wrote *The Calm Digital Life* as a practical guide to safer online decisions, without making the reader feel foolish for needing help or afraid of using technology.

If a message creates urgency, what is the first thing you check?
