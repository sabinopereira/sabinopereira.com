About

One creator. Two identities. Connected worlds.

Hi, I’m Sabino Pereira (SP), an independent creator building original worlds through books, music and meaningful products.

Under the creative umbrella SP × RB, we explore the deep integration of written narrative and auditory atmospheres.

"SP writes the stories. RB (Reira Bin) gives them a soundtrack. Together, they create worlds worth returning to."

The work

Ideas that move between forms.

Under SP, I write novels, essays and thought-provoking stories exploring identity, relationships, faith, healing and the moments that shape who we become. Under RB, I compose cinematic music, instrumental albums and soundtracks designed to evoke emotion, memory and imagination.

A story might become an album. An album might become a book. A book might inspire artwork or apparel. I build connected experiences that people can read, hear, wear and remember.

Mission

  • Stories you can read
  • Music you can feel
  • Experiences you’ll want to return to
  • Meaningful objects designed to be lived with
